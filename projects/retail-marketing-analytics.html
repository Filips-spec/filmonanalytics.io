Retail & Marketing Analytics

Customer Demographics and Marketing Channel Effectiveness

Client: 2Market (Retail Distribution Company)
Dataset: 2,209 Customer Records

Project Overview

2Market is a retail distribution company that needed to understand their customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and which marketing channels are more successful. I analyzed 2,209 customer records to uncover actionable insights that would optimize their marketing strategy.

Toolkit

Excel
Data cleaning, initial exploration, and outlier detection
SQL
Data merging, querying, and advanced analysis across multiple datasets
Tableau
Interactive dashboard creation for data visualization and stakeholder presentation

Methodology

Data Preparation & Cleaning
Imported the marketing dataset into Excel for initial assessment. Standardized inconsistent categorical data in Education and Marital Status fields. Identified and removed statistical outliers in Income and Age features to ensure data integrity. Validated data quality across 2,209 customer records.
Analysis Process
Conducted analysis across demographic features (age, education, marital status, income, country). Used SQL to merge customer data with advertising campaign data from social networks, brochures, and bulk email. Calculated lead conversion rates for each marketing channel. Performed segmentation analysis by demographics to identify high-performing customer groups and channels.
Dashboard Development
Designed an interactive Tableau dashboard optimized for large-scale data visualization. The dashboard emphasizes clear visual hierarchy with purposeful color schemes that support data storytelling, strategic feature selection highlighting the most actionable insights, concise titles and labels for immediate comprehension, and interactive filters enabling stakeholders to explore data independently.

Key Insights

  • Customer Demographics
    • Core customer base concentrated in 40s-60s age range
    • 50% are married or in relationships
    • Highly educated: 50% graduate degrees, 20% PhDs, 15% Master's degrees
    • Geographic: 50% from Spain, 15% South Africa, 10% Canada
  • Purchase Patterns
    • Liquor dominates at 50% of sales, followed by meat (27%)
    • 50% of customers have children, yet kid-oriented products show minimal sales
    • Indicates a potential market opportunity for family-oriented product lines
  • Marketing Channel Performance
    • Digital channels significantly outperformed traditional marketing
    • Social media and email campaigns achieved 5x higher conversion rates vs. brochures
    • Strong case for reallocating budget from print to digital channels

Business Recommendations

1
Shift marketing investment to digital channels
The conversion data shows clear superiority of social media and email over traditional brochures.
2
Explore family-oriented product lines
Approximately 50% of customers have children, yet kid-focused products are underrepresented in current sales.
3
Refine targeting strategy
Focus on the core demographic (educated professionals aged 40-60) through the digital channels where they're most responsive.
